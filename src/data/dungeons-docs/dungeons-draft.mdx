## Aldnzark Vault

West of Dragon Bridge stands Aldnzark Vault, a forgotten Dwemer tower now occupied by Hulgaar and his bandits. Drawn by the promise of rare geode minerals, their mining efforts are stalled by relentless Dwemer automatons. Unbeknownst to them, a powerful relic lies hidden deep within, fiercely protected by its ancient guardians.

### Rewards

- **Zanrath's Forge**: Aldnzark Cavern, in the boss chamber's chest; requires defeating Zanrath the Unyelding Sphere

### Tags

[Mini-Boss, Dwemer, Short]

## Temple of Ankarith

Temple of Ankarith - A nordic ruin located down west of Avanchazel, entry is at Coldfear Cavern.

### Rewards

- **Hammet's Talon of Dominion**: in a chest in the Coldfear Hallway of Trials last section; on the left just before Coldfear Hall of Vulon door
- **Hammet's Stalwart Stride**: in a chest on the left of Vulon's flaming throne in Coldfear Hall of Vulon
- **Hammet's Aegis of the North**: in a chest in Coldfear Portal Chamber area, beneath the serpent nordic symbol
- **Hammet's Wyrmhelm of Vigilance**: defeat Ankarith the Forgotten in Ankarith's Tomb and loot it from his body

### Notes

Most of the dungeon mini-bosses use ice magic and stalhrim
The trials are 4 obligatory mini-bosses to face; you need to defeat each of them to obtain their key
Before the Temple of Ankarith 3 dragons will face you in an open area (you can ignore them)
Ankarith the Forgotten will be the last boss of the temple; it uses lightning spells

### Tags

[Nordic ruin, dragon priest, dragons, mini-bosses and big bosses, HUGE, very difficult]
